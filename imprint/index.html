This website sets no cookies, uses no tracking or analytics, and loads no content from third parties. Fonts, stylesheets and scripts are served from the same server as the page itself.

Hosting on GitHub Pages

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P Kelly Jr St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit it, GitHub may process your IP address and other technical access data in server logs. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, the legitimate interest in operating a publicly accessible website. Details: GitHub Privacy Statement.

Contact by email

If you write me an email, I process the information you provide in order to answer your enquiry and to prepare a possible engagement. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Local storage

When you switch language, the website saves that preference in your browser’s localStorage. No personal data is stored, and the setting never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time in your browser settings.
